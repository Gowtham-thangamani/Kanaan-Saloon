Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This policy explains how Kanaan Gents Salon & Spa ("Kanaan", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you visit our website, our houses, or otherwise interact with us.

1. What we collect

When you book online or contact us, we collect: name, phone number, email (if provided), branch preference, service preference, message contents, and technical metadata (browser, device type, marketing source / UTM tags).

2. Why we use it

We use your data to confirm and manage your bookings, contact you about your appointment, improve our services, and — with your consent — for marketing communications. UTM and source data is used to measure marketing performance.

3. Who we share with

We do not sell your data. We share necessary information with our hosting provider, customer relationship management system, WhatsApp Cloud (Meta), and our analytics tools (Google Analytics, Meta, TikTok, Snapchat) under appropriate data-processing agreements.

4. Cookies & tracking

Our site uses essential cookies (for site function) and, with your consent, analytics and marketing cookies. You can change your preferences at any time via the cookie settings.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent for marketing communications. Contact kanaansaloon@gmail.com.

6. Retention

Booking and customer records are retained for the period required by UAE law and our legitimate business needs. Marketing data is retained until you withdraw consent.

7. Contact
