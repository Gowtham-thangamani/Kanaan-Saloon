Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: April 2026

Kanaan is committed to making this website usable for everyone, including people with visual, motor, hearing, or cognitive disabilities.

Our standard

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. This includes:

  • Sufficient colour contrast for text and interactive elements
  • Keyboard navigation across the entire site
  • Visible focus indicators on every focusable element
  • Semantic HTML, ARIA labels for icon-only controls
  • Alt text on every image
  • Honoured prefers-reduced-motion for users who prefer reduced animation
  • Right-to-left language support for Arabic
  • Forms with bound labels and accessible error messages

Known limitations

We are continuously improving. If you find an accessibility barrier, please let us know — your feedback shapes our priorities.

Contact us

If you experience difficulty using the site, email kanaansaloon@gmail.com or WhatsApp us — we will do our best to provide the information or service you need in an accessible format.

In our salons

Most Kanaan houses are accessible by lift or ground-floor entry. If you have specific access needs, contact your branch in advance and we will arrange.
