Common questions

Maybe we've already answered.

Most enquiries we get fall into one of these. Faster than a form.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking form (about 60 seconds), call +971 50 555 6795, or message us on WhatsApp. The branch confirms within 30 minutes during open hours.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — free up to 2 hours before your appointment. Reply to the WhatsApp confirmation message, or use the "Reschedule / Cancel" button on your booking confirmation page.

What are your prices?

Service starting prices appear on each service page and on the booking form. Final pricing depends on hair length, products, and add-ons — the branch confirms total before any work begins.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — but Friday evenings, weekends, and the hour before close are usually fully booked. Booking online or by phone first guarantees a chair.

I'd like to apply for a job

Use the careers page — it accepts your CV plus a portfolio link. We respond within a week.
