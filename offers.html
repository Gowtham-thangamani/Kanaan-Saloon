    Frequently asked

    About our offers.

    Are these prices final?

    Yes — the listed price is exactly what you pay at the chair. VAT is included, there's no booking fee, and there's no upsell during your visit.

    Can I use an offer at any branch?

    The Complete Grooming Package is available at every Kanaan branch. The 8 Premium Spa Services package runs at our spa branches — Baniyas Spa, Khalifa City, Al Ain, Khalidiya, Old Shahamah and VIP Muroor.

    How do I cancel or reschedule?

    Free up to 2 hours before your appointment. Just reply to the WhatsApp confirmation, or use the Reschedule / Cancel link on your booking confirmation page.

    Do you accept walk-ins for these offers?

    Yes, where capacity allows. Booking online or by phone first guarantees a chair — especially on Friday evenings and weekends.
