Integrations

Tracking pixels, booking webhook, and CRM connections.

Tracking IDs

These power GA4, Meta, TikTok, and Snapchat tracking. Pixels only fire after the user accepts marketing cookies.

Booking webhook

When a guest submits a booking, the form POSTs JSON to this URL. Recommended: a Make.com or Zapier scenario that fans out to email + Google Sheet + WhatsApp Cloud + your CRM.

CRM adapter

Choose which CRM/booking system the webhook should forward to. Switching here changes the Make.com scenario template you'll use — no front-end changes needed.

Make.com scenario blueprint

A ready-to-import Make.com scenario that wires the booking webhook to email, Google Sheet, and WhatsApp Cloud. Import it into Make.com and connect your accounts.
