Change password

Updates the password on your Supabase admin account. To add or remove admins, use the Supabase dashboard → Authentication → Users.

  • At least 8 characters
  • At least one letter (A–Z)
  • At least one number (0–9)
  • At least one symbol (! @ # $ etc)
  • Different from your current password
