Page edits

Change individual texts, headings and images on any page — English or Arabic — without touching the layout, code or SEO settings.

Pick a page and load it — every text passage and image appears as its own field. Change what you need and Save & publish; everything you leave alone stays exactly as it is. Clearing a field back to the original removes the edit. If the same short text appears several times on one page (e.g. a repeated button label), editing it changes every occurrence on that page. Blog articles are edited in the Blog panel instead.
