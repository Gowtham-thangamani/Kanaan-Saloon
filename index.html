Common questions

Before you call.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking form (about 60 seconds), call +971 50 555 6795, or message us on WhatsApp. The branch confirms within 30 minutes during open hours.

What are your prices?

Each branch has its own menu — see the Services & Prices page to browse by branch. Cuts start at 25 AED at Baniyas Barber and 30–50 AED at most other houses. VAT included.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — free up to 2 hours before your appointment. Reply to the WhatsApp confirmation, or use the Reschedule / Cancel button on your booking confirmation page.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — but Friday evenings, weekends, and the last hour before close fill up fast. Booking online or by phone first guarantees a chair.

What time do you close?

All branches close at 23:00 daily. Last bookings accepted ~60 minutes before close for shorter services. VIP Muroor opens at 11:00 (not 10:00 like the others); Friday hours start at 14:00 across the chain.

More questions? Visit the contact page or message us on WhatsApp.
