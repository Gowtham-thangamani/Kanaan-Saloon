Our Story

A branch built on patience, posture, and precision.

The Beginning

Founded in Abu Dhabi.
Made for its gentlemen.

Kanaan began with a simple frustration — that proper, considered grooming for men was harder to find than it should have been in our city. We opened our first chair to fix it.

Today we operate ten branches across Abu Dhabi and Al Ain. Some are spa-led. Some are master-barber rooms. One is a private VIP suite. All of them are held to the same brief: take the work seriously, treat the guest properly, never cut a corner the customer can feel.

What Makes Us Different

Three things, held without compromise.

01

Master Craftsmanship

Our barbers and therapists train continuously. Technique is taught and audited — not assumed.

02

Spa-Grade Hygiene

Disposable single-use blades, sterilised tools, sealed packs. The standard is medical, not cosmetic.

03

Time Honoured

No rushed cuts. No clock-watching. The chair is yours for the time the work requires.

Standards

Standards you can see.

We list our hygiene and product standards openly because trust is built in the small details — the things you notice when no one is watching.

  • — Single-use blades and disposables on every chair
  • — Tools sterilised between every guest
  • — Professional product lines only — never private-label substitutes
  • — Continuous staff training across all 10 branches
  • — Bilingual service, English and Arabic
