This page explains the cookies and similar technologies we use on kanaanspa.ae and how to manage them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. They help us run the site, remember your preferences, and measure how our marketing performs.

Categories we use

1. Strictly necessary

These keep the site working — language preference, your booking step in progress, and your cookie-consent choice itself. They cannot be disabled.

2. Analytics

Anonymous usage measurement via Google Analytics 4 / Google Tag Manager. We use this to understand which pages convert and where to improve. Loaded only if you accept analytics cookies.

3. Marketing

Pixels from Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, Snapchat, and Google Ads. These help us measure and optimise our paid advertising. Loaded only if you accept marketing cookies.

How to change your choices

Use the cookie banner that appears on your first visit, or click the link below to reset your preferences. After resetting, the banner will appear again on your next page load.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Note that some essential cookies are required for the site to function, so blocking everything may cause issues with the booking funnel.
