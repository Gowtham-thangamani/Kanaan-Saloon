Frequently Asked

Common questions.

Will keratin damage my hair?

Done correctly with professional formulas, no — it deposits protein into the hair shaft. We use formaldehyde-free formulations and the result lasts 3–4 months.

How do I know if I need a protein treatment?

Signs include: hair feeling rough, breaking easily, looking dull, or losing elasticity (stretches without bouncing back). Book a consultation if unsure.

Can I treat hair loss at Kanaan?

We offer scalp therapy and a hair-loss consultation. We will tell you honestly what we can and can't address — and refer to a trichologist if appropriate.

How long does a hair treatment take?

Protein treatment: 60 minutes. Keratin smoothing: 2–3 hours. Scalp therapy: 45 minutes. Hair loss consultation: 30 minutes.
