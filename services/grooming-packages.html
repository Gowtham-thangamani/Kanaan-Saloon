Frequently Asked

Common questions.

Do I have to do all services in one visit?

Yes — packages are designed as a single curated visit. If you prefer to spread services across visits, book each individually instead.

Can I customise a package?

Yes — speak to us via WhatsApp and we will build a custom package around what you actually need. Common requests include swapping a service or adding a Moroccan bath.

Are package prices final?

Yes — the package price is what you pay. No surprise add-ons. If you request something not in the package, we tell you the additional price before doing it.

Which package is the best value?

The 8-Service Signature Package at 250 AED is the highest value. The 6-Service Essential at 100 AED is the most affordable entry point.
